Our Mission

Turning extreme biology
into real solutions

Caribbean hot springs host microbial communities that have spent thousands of years evolving in conditions hostile to almost every other form of life. That pressure produces biology that is genuinely unlike anything found in conventional environments.

We are building systematic methods to study, collect, and translate that biology into high-value products for the pharmaceutical, agricultural, and biotechnology sectors — while working closely with Caribbean governments and research partners to do it responsibly.

Our work is proprietary and conducted under formal IP protection. Specific methods and target organisms are disclosed only under NDA to qualified partners.

Novel Plasmid DNA

Extremophile microorganisms carry plasmids encoding traits absent in conventional lab strains. We are developing a catalogue of these sequences for biopharmaceutical, gene therapy, and research applications.

Biopharma · Gene Therapy
💊

Antibiotic Discovery

Microbes in competitive extreme environments produce antimicrobial compounds to survive. We are screening novel isolates for activity against drug-resistant pathogens including those on WHO priority pathogen lists.

AMR · Drug Discovery
🌿

Environmental Applications

Microbial communities from sulfur-rich, metal-laden spring environments have demonstrated capabilities relevant to bioremediation and sustainable industrial processes. Details under NDA.

Bioremediation · Industry
